Welcome to Harry Potter

Harry Potter is a fantasy universe created by British author J.K. Rowling, first introduced through the book series in 1997. It follows a young wizard's journey through Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry and has since expanded into films, theme parks, and spin-off stories.

About Harry Potter

Set primarily at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, the series follows young wizard Harry Potter as he learns magic, forms lasting friendships, and confronts a dark wizard responsible for his parents' death.

Beyond the seven core books, the franchise has grown into eight feature films, a stage play, spin-off movies, and one of the most visited fictional universes in the world — complete with theme parks and museum exhibits dedicated to its magic.
